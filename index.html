Questions

Frequently asked

Is the domain really included in every hosting plan?

Yes. Each yearly plan, from Bronze to Professional, includes one free domain registration. Ask us if you already own a domain and want to move it.

Can you build a system in Dari as well as English?

Yes. Our software and websites are delivered in Dari, Pashto and English as needed, including right-to-left screens and reports.

Do you work outside Herat?

Yes. We are headquartered in Herat and have delivered projects and training for clients across Afghanistan, including Nimroz and Kabul-based organisations.

What happens after the software is delivered?

We train your staff, then continue with maintenance and support. Hosting and updates can be included in an annual agreement.
