About Herat Host

Our customers, our marketers.

Herat Host IT Services Co. has delivered projects for private companies and NGOs for more than a decade, earning a record as a reliable provider of IT training, software and database development, and website design. Based in Herat and working across Afghanistan, our contract history includes international and national NGOs, public-sector bodies and private firms — including advanced QuickBooks training for PPA sectors in partnership with ABADE.
